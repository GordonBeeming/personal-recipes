#### **Ingredients**

**For the Pork Belly:**

* Pork Belly, skin-on
* 2 tbsp Sea Salt (fine or flaked)
* 1 tsp Black Pepper
* Olive Oil (optional, for the meat side)

**For the Orange Roast Veg & Parsnips:**

* 350g Baby Parsnips, washed and halved if thick
* 500g Pumpkin (Kent or Butternut), cut into chunks
* 500g Sweet Potato, cut into chunks
* 2-3 large Carrots, peeled and sliced into thick rounds
* 2 tbsp Olive Oil
* Salt and Pepper to taste

**For the Skin-on Rustic Mash:**

* 1 kg Potatoes (Dutch Cream or Yukon Gold), scrubbed
* 50g Butter
* 1/4 cup Milk (adjust for desired consistency)
* Salt for boiling water

#### **Instructions**

1. **Prep the Pork:**
   * Pat the pork belly skin completely dry with paper towels.
   * Using a sharp knife, score the skin into small squares, cutting almost down to the meat but not through it.
   * Rub the salt and pepper generously into the gaps and over the surface of the skin.


2. **The Air Fryer Blast:**
   * Place the pork belly in the air fryer.
   * Cook at **200°C** for 20 minutes to get the crackling started.
   * After 20 minutes, reduce the temperature to **160°C** and cook for **30-35 minutes per kg** (e.g., 500g = 15-18 minutes, 2kg = 60-70 minutes, etc).
   * The meat should be tender and the crackling crispy when done.


3. **Roast the Vegetables:**
   * While the pork starts, preheat your oven to **200°C**.
   * Toss the pumpkin, sweet potato, carrots, and baby parsnips in olive oil and salt.
   * Spread them out on a large baking tray—ensure they aren't crowded so they roast rather than steam!
   * Roast for 35-45 minutes, shaking the tray halfway through.


4. **Make the Rustic Mash:**
   * Cut the scrubbed potatoes (skins on!) into small 2 cm cubes.
   * Place in a pot of cold salted water and bring to a boil.
   * Simmer for 12-15 minutes until fork-tender.
   * Drain well, then add butter and milk. Mash until you have a textured, rustic mash.


5. **Rest and Serve:**
   * Once the pork is done, remove it from the air fryer and let it rest for **10 minutes**.
   * Slice the pork along your pre-scored lines into perfect squares.
   * Serve alongside the orange veg medley, baby parsnips, and the skin-on mash.



#### **Serving Suggestions**

This roast is a classic Sunday meal that pairs beautifully with a rich gravy or even a dollop of apple sauce. For a bit of extra flavor in your mash, you can infuse the butter with a crushed garlic clove or fresh herbs like thyme while the potatoes are boiling.
