#### **Ingredients**

**For the Lamb:**

* ~1.6 kg Boneless Lamb Leg
* 1 tbsp Olive Oil (for searing)
* 500 ml Beef or Lamb Stock
* 1 large Onion, halved
* 4-5 cloves Garlic, smashed
* 2 sprigs Fresh Rosemary (or 1 tsp dried)
* 1 tbsp Tomato Paste
* 1 tbsp Worcestershire Sauce
* Salt and Black Pepper to taste

**For the Carrots:**

* 4-6 large Carrots, peeled and cut into thick chunks
* Salt and Pepper (to season when adding to the pot)

**For the Butter Mash:**

* 1.2 kg Potatoes (Dutch Cream or Sebago), peeled and cubed
* 60g Butter
* ~1/3 cup Milk (adjust for desired consistency)
* Salt for boiling water

#### **Instructions**

1. **Sear the Lamb:**
   * Preheat your oven to **150°C**.
   * Season the lamb leg generously with salt and pepper.
   * Heat oil in a heavy, oven-safe pot over medium-high heat. Brown the lamb on all sides until deep golden brown—this builds the "beefy" savory flavour. 🥩


2. **Set up the Braise:**
   * Remove the pot from heat. Add the onion, garlic, rosemary, tomato paste, and Worcestershire sauce.
   * Pour in the stock until it reaches about a third of the way up the meat.
   * Place a double layer of foil tightly over the pot, then press the lid on top to create a perfect seal.


3. **The Slow Cook:**
   * Place the pot in the oven at **150°C**.
   * Cook for **3 hours**. 


4. **Add the Carrots:**
   * Carefully remove the pot from the oven and lift the foil (watch out for the steam! 💨).
   * Tuck the carrot chunks into the liquid around the lamb. 🥕
   * Re-seal the foil and lid tightly and return to the oven for another **1.5 to 2 hours**.
   * The lamb is ready when it pulls apart easily with two forks (Internal temp around 95°C-98°C).


5. **Make the Mash:**
   * While the lamb finishes its final hour, boil the cubed potatoes in salted water for 15-20 minutes until very tender.
   * Drain well, then add butter and milk. Mash until silky smooth.


6. **Rest and Shred:**
   * Remove the lamb from the pot and let it rest, covered, for **20 minutes**.
   * While resting, you can simmer the remaining pot juices on the stove to thicken into a rich gravy.
   * Shred the lamb with two forks and serve it over a big scoop of mash with those soft carrots on the side. 🍽️



#### **Serving Suggestions**

This dish is all about the gravy—make sure you spoon plenty of the braising liquid over the shredded meat and mash. If you want a bit of extra "zing," a small dollop of mint sauce on the side cuts through the richness of the lamb perfectly. 🌿
