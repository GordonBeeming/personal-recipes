#### **Ingredients**

**For the Chicken:**
* 1.5 kg Whole Chicken
* 2 tbsp Olive Oil or Melted Butter
* 1 tsp Sea Salt
* 1/2 tsp Black Pepper
* Fresh herbs (like thyme or rosemary), finely chopped

**For the Roast Veg:**
* 1 kg Potatoes (like Dutch Cream or Sebago), peeled and cut into chunks
* 500g Mixed Roasting Veg (like carrots and pumpkin), peeled and cut into chunks
* 2 tbsp Olive Oil
* Salt and Pepper to taste

#### **Instructions**

1. **Prep the Chicken:**
   * Take the chicken out of the fridge about 20-30 minutes early to take the chill off. 
   * Preheat your oven to **200°C**.
   * Pat the chicken *completely* dry inside and out with paper towels. 🍗
   * Gently slide your fingers under the skin at the main cavity, sweeping side to side to separate it from the breast meat. 
   * Massage half of your oil/butter and herb mixture under the skin directly onto the meat, and rub the rest all over the outside. Season generously with salt and pepper.

2. **The Roast:**
   * Place the chicken in a large roasting pan and put it in the oven. 
   * A 1.5 kg bird will take about **1 hour and 15 minutes** (roughly 20 mins per 500g, plus an extra 15 mins).

3. **Roast the Vegetables:**
   * Toss your chopped potatoes and roasting veg in olive oil, salt, and pepper. 🥔
   * You can add these directly to the chicken's roasting pan to soak up the juices, or spread them on a separate baking tray. 
   * Put them in the oven for the final **45-50 minutes** of the chicken's cooking time, shaking the tray halfway through so they get golden and crispy on all sides.

4. **Rest the Bird:**
   * Once the chicken is deeply golden and cooked through (the juices should run clear when you pierce the thickest part of the thigh), remove it from the oven. 
   * Transfer it to a board, tent it loosely with foil, and let it rest for **10-15 minutes**. Don't skip this—it keeps the meat beautifully juicy!

5. **Serve:**
   * Carve the rested chicken and serve it up with the crispy roast veg. 🍽️

#### **Serving Suggestions**

Make sure you pour any of those resting juices from the cutting board back over the sliced chicken. If you have time, whisking together a quick gravy from the pan drippings takes this whole meal to the next level!
